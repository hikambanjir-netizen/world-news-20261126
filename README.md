The World Today — 26 November 2026. A one-page front-page summary of
Thursday 26 November 2026's world news, drawn from scratch in
pure-stdlib Python: a hand-made 5x7 bitmap font, a small raster canvas
with a pictogram per story, and a from-scratch PNG encoder.

Lead story: tariff shock rocks markets — Trump unveils sweeping new
tariffs on China, Japan, the EU and Taiwan, and the Nasdaq sinks
almost 4% as an AI-stock rout rattles Wall Street. Also inside: Brent
crude tops $94 after the Houthis claim a strike on Saudi Arabia's Ras
Tanura refinery; the first Pakistani astronaut, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali,
reaches the Tiangong station aboard Shenzhou-24; and 31 are killed in
a fuel-tanker fire on a Cairo highway. A ticker strip carries the
rest of the day: an Israeli strike kills a senior Hamas official in
Gaza, Russia's own figures show nearly 500,000 war dead in Ukraine,
and US tariffs hit 14 trading partners.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, ~20 KB). No network, no
clock reads, no unseeded randomness — two runs write byte-identical
output.
